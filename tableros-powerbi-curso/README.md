# Power BI Dashboards — Course Projects

Three interactive dashboards built in Power BI Desktop as part of the Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst course. Open each `.pbix` in Power BI Desktop to explore them (slicers, maps, and AI visuals are all interactive).

## 1. World Population Forecast (`Forecast_10_cs0a.pbix`)
Forecasting + geospatial analysis on world population data.
- **ArcGIS map** of current population by country/territory
- **Line chart with forecast** — population trend by year with projected values
- **Column chart** by continent + KPI card (current population)
- *Skills: forecasting, ArcGIS maps, KPI cards*

## 2. Sales Time-Series Analysis (`Ale_Time-series-analysis_11_ilo4.pbix`)
Time-series analysis on retail sales (Date × Product × Sales model).
- **Line chart** — Sales Amount over time, split by product Category
- **Scatter chart** — Order Quantity vs Sales Amount across dates (outlier spotting)
- Custom time-series visual
- *Skills: time intelligence, trend analysis, scatter plots*

## 3. Vehicle CO₂ Emissions — Key Drivers (`CO2-emissions-by-vehicle_12_t1js.pbix`)
AI-powered root-cause analysis on vehicle emissions data.
- **Key influencers visual** — which factors (engine size, fuel type, powertrain, transmission) drive CO₂ emissions up or down
- **Decomposition tree** — interactive drill-down of emissions by engine-size bins, fuel type, and powertrain
- **Card** — vehicle count
- *Skills: AI visuals (key influencers, decomposition tree), driver analysis*
