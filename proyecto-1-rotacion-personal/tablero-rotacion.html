Key findings

2.4×

Overtime is the #1 driver. 14.5% attrition with overtime vs 6.0% without — the single strongest signal in the dataset.

11.8%

Sales Representatives are the highest-risk role, followed by HR Managers (11.3%). Sales is also the highest-attrition department (9.3%).

11.4%

Youngest employees leave most (ages 18–25), vs just 5.0% for ages 46–55 — early-career retention is the weak point.

12.9%

Worst work-life balance triples down on risk: employees rating it 1 of 4 leave at 12.9%.

10.9%

Years 2–3 are the danger zone. After year 10, attrition falls to 5.5% — loyalty compounds with tenure.

−$325/mo

Leavers earn less than stayers on average ($6,225 vs $6,550 monthly) — pay sits quietly behind the louder signals.
