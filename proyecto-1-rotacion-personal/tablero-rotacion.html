Key findings

  • Overtime is the #1 driver: employees who work overtime leave at 14.5% vs 6.0% for those who don't — a 2.4× higher rate.
  • Sales Representatives are the highest-risk role (11.8%), followed by HR Managers (11.3%).
  • Youngest employees leave most: ages 18–25 show 11.4% attrition, vs 5.0% for ages 46–55.
  • Poor work-life balance triples the risk at the lowest rating (12.9%).
  • The danger zone is years 2–3 (10.9%); employees with 11+ years are the most loyal (5.5%).
  • Leavers earn less: $6,225 avg. monthly income vs $6,550 for those who stay.
